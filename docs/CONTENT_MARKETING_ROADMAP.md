## 1. Editorial Mission & Content Pillars

SiteCommons's content engine is not designed to produce superficial, generic "top 10 productivity tips" articles. Its mission is to build the **internet's most authoritative, engineering-grade resource for construction project managers, civil engineers, quantity surveyors, and trade subcontractors**.

Every piece of content must solve a high-stakes, expensive operational problem and showcase how SiteCommons delivers structural certainty.

### The Six Content Pillars:

```
                           ┌───────────────────────────────┐
                           │    TASKPILOT KNOWLEDGE HUB   │
                           └───────────────┬───────────────┘
                                           │
     ┌──────────────┬──────────────┬───────┴──────┬──────────────┬──────────────┐
     ▼              ▼              ▼              ▼              ▼              ▼
┌─────────┐    ┌─────────┐    ┌─────────┐    ┌─────────┐    ┌─────────┐    ┌─────────┐
│Pillar 1 │    │Pillar 2 │    │Pillar 3 │    │Pillar 4 │    │Pillar 5 │    │Pillar 6 │
│Jobsite  │    │Financial│    │Quality &│    │Tech &   │    │Subby    │    │Software │
│Ops &    │    │Control &│    │Safety   │    │Mobile   │    │Cashflow │    │Buyer    │
│Field    │    │EVM / BOQ│    │Hold-    │    │Offline  │    │& Claims │    │Teardowns│
│Execution│    │Variance │    │Points   │    │Engine   │    │Defense  │    │& Reviews│
└─────────┘    └─────────┘    └─────────┘    └─────────┘    └─────────┘    └─────────┘
```

1. **Pillar 1: Jobsite Operations & Field Execution:** Daily progress reporting (DPR), site diary workflows, trade muster management, equipment logbook discipline, and supervisor handover best practices.
2. **Pillar 2: Financial Control & BOQ Earned Value Management:** Quantity surveying, EVM formulas (CPI, SPI, EAC, VAC), schedule-to-budget synchronization, and material wastage control.
3. **Pillar 3: Quality Control, Inspections & Defect Prevention:** Inspection & Test Plans (ITP), 3-step hold points, concrete pre-pour sign-offs, waterproofing verification, and punch list elimination.
4. **Pillar 4: Construction Technology, Mobility & Offline Field Operations:** GPS geofencing ethics/compliance, battery conservation in field apps, low-bandwidth sync architectures, and field-to-office automation.
5. **Pillar 5: Subcontractor Survival & Dispute Protection:** Payment claim documentation, timestamped photo evidence defense against backcharges, change order tracking, and statutory compliance.
6. **Pillar 6: Software Buyer Guides & Competitor Teardowns:** Unbiased SaaS cost calculators, per-user pricing exposure, feature matrices, and migration guides from legacy or generic tools.

   - Convert core diagrams, formulas, and workflows into 8–12 slide visual PDF carousels.
   - Example: *"5 EVM formulas every Project Director must know"* generates an average 4.2% engagement rate on LinkedIn.

   - Record high-speed 2-minute walkthroughs solving the exact problem in SiteCommons (e.g. *"Generating a 4-trade daily progress report on an iPhone in 90 seconds"*).

4. **Sales Enablement Battle Cards:**
   - Summarize competitor comparison teardowns into 1-page PDF comparison matrices used by sales and onboarding reps during prospect discovery calls.
5. **Interactive Tool & Template Download Hub:**
