## 1. Search Engine Optimization Strategy & Overview

TaskPilot's SEO architecture is engineered to bypass high-cost, hyper-competitive broad head terms (e.g., *"project management software"* with Keyword Difficulty 95+ and $55 CPC) and dominate high-intent, bottom-of-the-funnel commercial keywords where buyers have urgent purchase intent.

### Strategic Keyword Segmentation:
1. **Tier 1: Core Commercial & Category Keywords** — Contractors actively searching for operational and field software.
2. **Tier 2: Jobsite Pain Point & Solution Keywords** — Contractors seeking cures for specific operational failures (phantom labor, late daily reports, unverified punch lists, budget overruns).
3. **Tier 3: Competitor Switcher & Alternative Keywords** — Prospects seeking cheaper or field-native replacements for Procore, Fieldwire, Asana, Monday, and ClickUp.
4. **Tier 4: Persona & Industry Vertical Keywords** — General contractors, MEP trades, civil engineers, real estate builders.
5. **Tier 5: Informational High-Trust Knowledge Queries** — Educational search queries for construction formulas (EVM, CPI, SPI), templates, and compliance guides that feed the top-of-funnel conversion engine.

## 4. Technical On-Page SEO Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Every published URL across the TaskPilot marketing site adheres to strict programmatic SEO standards:

1. **Title Tag Structure:** `[Primary Keyword] — [Compelling Value Proposition / Differentiator] | TaskPilot` (Target: 50–60 characters).
2. **Meta Description Structure:** `[Problem statement] + [TaskPilot specific solution with metric] + [CTA].` (Target: 145–158 characters).
3. **Canonical URLs:** Hardcoded self-referencing absolute canonical URL (`https://taskpilot.app/...`).
4. **Heading Hierarchy:**
   - Single `<h1>` containing primary target keyword and primary benefit.
   - Multiple `<h2>` tags structuring key features, use cases, FAQs, and comparison matrices.
   - `<h3>` tags structuring card modules and specific sub-points.
5. **Schema.org Structured Data:**
   - Software pages inject `SoftwareApplication` + `Organization`.
   - Comparison and FAQ pages inject `FAQPage` with min. 5 verified Q&A pairs.
   - Blog posts inject `BlogPosting` or `Article` with author, publisher, and date published.
   - Global pages inject `BreadcrumbList` reflecting logical IA hierarchy.
6. **Internal Linking Rules:**
   - Every feature page links to at least 2 relevant solution pages and 1 comparison page.
   - Every blog post links to at least 2 feature pages and 1 primary product CTA.
   - Anchor text uses exact or partial match target keywords (never generic "click here" or "learn more").
